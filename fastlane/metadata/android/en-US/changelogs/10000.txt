First stable release.

- Bluetooth mesh messaging with no accounts, servers, or tracking
- DMs, public channels, private groups, invite-only rooms, bulletin boards
- Photos, video, voice notes, and live push-to-talk over the mesh
- bitchat compatible, relaying across up to 7 hops
- Optional Nostr bridging beyond Bluetooth range, with Tor routing
- Offline Cashu ecash transfers, no internet needed on either phone
- Panic wipe erases keys and messages with a triple tap
